AI Knowledge Domains
Personal Taxonomy

A practitioner-first framework for organizing research, building expertise, and positioning advisory work. Built from the perspective of what to DO with AI, not what the field IS. Designed to guide 2–6 week research sprints and specialization decisions.

Design intent Deliberately non-MECE. Overlap zones between categories are the most productive research areas — not defects to eliminate. The framework separates four orientations; the intersections are where practitioner differentiation lives.
Domain 01
Engineering AI Systems
Design, build, and operate AI-native capabilities — the craft of making AI products work reliably in the real world.
Core sub-domains
AI Agents & Pipelines Prompt Engineering Context & Memory Architecture RAG & Retrieval Systems Multi-agent Orchestration Tool Use & MCP Model Selection & Evaluation Fine-tuning & Adaptation
Supporting foundations (assumed literacy)
LLMs / NLP Deep Learning basics API integration
Context & memory engineering belongs here as a first-class sub-domain, not an infrastructure concern. Deciding what goes in the context window, what is stored externally, and how memory persists across sessions is a core product engineering decision that determines whether an AI system is useful or not.
Domain 02
Enterprise AI
Organizational deployment, institutionalization, and governance of AI across business functions and workflows at scale.
Core sub-domains
AI Strategy & Roadmapping Workflow Integration Change Management AI Governance & Risk Feasibility × Impact Prioritization Organizational Capability Building AI Maturity Models Regulatory Compliance (EU AI Act)
Current high-signal area
Agentic system deployment Workshop & enablement design
The deployment gap — 62% of enterprises experimenting, fewer than 10% scaled — means advisory work here far outpaces implementation. Strategy and change management are the bottlenecks, not the technology.
Domain 03
Eval Sets
Methods, frameworks, and tooling for measuring AI system performance and the quality of AI-assisted processes — a horizontal that cuts across all other domains.
Core sub-domains
Model Benchmark Design Task-specific Evaluation Agent Behavior Testing Human-in-the-loop Eval Process Quality Measurement Output Reliability Frameworks Red-teaming & Adversarial Testing Eval Dataset Curation
Why it's a standalone domain
Applies to model builders and deployers alike Cuts across all other domains
Standard taxonomies bury eval inside MLOps or Safety. That undersells it. Eval methodology is what distinguishes rigorous AI work from intuition-driven AI work — it applies whether you're building a model, deploying an agent, or measuring an AI-augmented SE process.
Domain 04
Engineering Systems through AI
Using AI as instrument to architect, design, verify, and develop systems — where the delivered artifact does not itself contain AI, but the engineering process was AI-augmented.
Core sub-domains
AI-augmented MBSE & SysML Document Intelligence (Arcadia, specs, standards) AI-assisted Requirements Engineering Verification & Validation with AI AI for Architecture Decision Support Supporting Systems for SE Processes Digital Twin & Simulation Augmentation
Proof case (WS2 engagement)
Arcadia vs SysML v2 document-reading product
The most counterintuitive framing in the SE community. "The solution doesn't contain AI" is a feature, not a gap — it separates AI capability from AI outcome. The SE community will resist AI adoption partly because they conflate these. This framing resolves the objection.
Intersections Productive Overlap Zones — Where Differentiation Lives
Domain 01 × 03
Eval for AI Products
Designing evaluation frameworks for agents and pipelines — how do you know your AI system is actually working? Methodology for testing tool use, multi-step reasoning, and context retention under realistic conditions.
Domain 01 × 02
Agentic Workflow Automation
The point where building AI systems (technical) meets deploying them in organizations (strategic). Agent architecture decisions that determine enterprise viability: latency, reliability, human oversight, and audit trails.
Domain 02 × 03
AI Adoption Measurement
How do organizations know their AI investment is working? Eval methodology applied to enterprise deployment — measuring process quality, productivity delta, and organizational capability change, not just model accuracy.
Domain 01 × 04
AI Tools for SE Practice
Engineering the AI systems that augment the SE process — the supporting systems (RAG pipelines, document readers, analysis agents) built specifically for MBSE workflows. The WS2 product thesis lives exactly here.
Domain 03 × 04
Process Quality in SE
Evaluating the quality of AI-augmented systems engineering outputs. Did the AI-assisted requirements analysis produce better requirements? How do you measure that rigorously? An almost entirely open research area.
Domain 02 × 04
AI Transformation of SE Orgs
The MBSE engagement context — deploying AI augmentation across a systems engineering organization. Strategy, change management, and SE domain knowledge all required simultaneously. The current client engagement.
Assumed background / outside active research scope
Foundational ML / deep learning literacy Transformer architecture basics Python for automation AI for Science (biology, materials, climate) Neuromorphic computing Embodied AI / robotics World models (watch, not research) AI economics (awareness, not specialization)
